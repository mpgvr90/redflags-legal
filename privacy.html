Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Who we are

RED FLAGS is a private relationship checklist and journal for Android and iOS. It is published by Tetiana Lahotska ("we", "us"). Contact: codexxdev77@gmail.com.

This policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes and what choices you have.

In short

  • Your relationships, flags, moments, journal, boundaries, promises and reflections stay on your device. We never receive them.
  • There is no account and no server of ours.
  • To check purchases and the free trial, the app uses RevenueCat. It receives an anonymous ID, purchase data, basic device information and your IP address, never your records.
  • Payments are made through Google Play or the App Store.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking. We do not sell data.

Data stored only on your device

Everything you write in the app is stored in a local database on your device: relationship profiles, flag observations, moments, journal entries, emotions, boundaries, promises and monthly reflections. Settings such as language, theme and reminders are stored on the device too.

If you turn on the app lock, your PIN is stored only as a salted hash in the device's secure storage. Biometric unlock is handled by your device; the app never sees your fingerprint or face data.

Exports (JSON, CSV, PDF) and encrypted backups are created only when you choose to make them, and go wherever you decide to save or share them. Backups are encrypted with a passphrase that only you know; we cannot recover it.

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on the device.

Purchases and access: RevenueCat

The app uses RevenueCat, Inc. to process in-app purchase receipts and to know whether you have access (free trial or lifetime access). RevenueCat acts as our service provider. It receives:

  • an anonymous app user ID generated by the app (not linked to your name or email);
  • transaction data: the product bought, purchase date, price, currency and store transaction identifiers, including the free trial product on iOS;
  • basic device information: device model, operating system and version, app version, language and store country;
  • your IP address, which is part of every network request and may be used to estimate your country.

RevenueCat does not receive your journal, your relationship records or anything else you write in the app.

RevenueCat is based in the United States, so this data may be processed outside your country. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

Payments: Google Play and the App Store

Payments are processed by Google Play (Android) or the Apple App Store (iOS) under their own terms and privacy policies. We never receive your card details or your store account email.

Free trial

On Android the free trial starts automatically at the first launch and lasts 14 days. The start date is the date RevenueCat first saw your anonymous app user ID.

On iOS you start a free 5-day trial yourself, through a free in-app purchase called "5-day Trial". The trial dates come from that transaction.

When the trial ends, the app locks until you buy lifetime access. There is no subscription and you are never charged automatically.

Why we process data (legal bases)

Where the EU or UK GDPR applies: purchase and trial data are processed to provide the access you are entitled to (performance of a contract) and to prevent abuse of the free trial (our legitimate interest). Records on your device are processed by the app on your device, at your request.

Retention

Data on your device stays until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. RevenueCat keeps purchase records for as long as needed to provide access and to meet accounting and legal obligations.

Your choices and rights

  • Erase everything stored by the app in Settings › Delete all data; uninstalling the app also removes it.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to complain to a data protection authority.
  • To ask about purchase data held by RevenueCat, write to codexxdev77@gmail.com. Because the ID is anonymous, we may ask for your store receipt to find your records.

No advertising, analytics or tracking

The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking tools. We do not sell or share personal data for advertising.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly process their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Significant changes will also be described in the app's release notes.
