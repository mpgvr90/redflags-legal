Delete the data on your device

Everything you write in RED FLAGS is stored only on your device. To delete it:

  1. Open RED FLAGS.
  2. Go to Settings › Delete all data.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm.

This erases every relationship, moment, flag, boundary, promise, journal entry and reflection stored on the device at once. Uninstalling the app removes all app data, including settings and the app lock PIN. Exports and backups you saved elsewhere are not touched; delete those files yourself.

Ask us to delete purchase data

RevenueCat, our service provider, holds an anonymous app user ID and your purchase records (product, date, price, store transaction IDs). To ask for them to be deleted:

  1. Write to codexxdev77@gmail.com with the subject "RED FLAGS data deletion".
  2. Attach your Google Play or App Store receipt for RED FLAGS, if you have one. The ID is anonymous, so the receipt is how we find your records.

We delete the records within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is kept

Deleting purchase data removes your access record: if you bought lifetime access, use Restore purchases later to bring it back from your store account. Google and Apple keep their own payment records under their own policies. We may keep a minimal record of a transaction where tax or accounting law requires it, for as long as that law requires.
